# Known limitations

- The 15-item catalog is too small for credible recommendation benchmarks.
- Demo scores are illustrative and not fetched from a ratings service.
- TF-IDF has limited understanding of synonyms, context, and nuance.
- Descriptions are manually authored and may omit important themes.
- There is no spoiler detection, personalization history, or authentication.
- Sentiment predictions are not exposed in the UI.
- No generative agent or external API is implemented yet.

Do not describe these planned capabilities as completed features.
